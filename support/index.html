How to play

Tap a station and your hands do the job: buns onto the board, sausages and patties onto the grill, fillings into buns, sauces over the food, cups under the soda tap (hold the lever to pour), fries into the fryer and pizza into the oven.

Tap a customer to hand over what is ready for them, or drag food to them. Sweep up the coins they leave and shoo the thieves away. Reach the day’s target to earn stars.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. It is not shared between devices, and deleting the app erases it. If you move to a new iPhone or iPad by restoring a backup, your progress comes with it.

How do I turn the sound off?

Open Settings (the gear button) and switch Sound, Music or Vibration off.

How do I see the tutorial again?

Open Settings and tap Reset next to Replay the tutorial.

Is there anything to buy?

No. There are no adverts and nothing to buy inside the game.

The game feels slow

Close other apps you are not using. The game also lowers its picture sharpness by itself when a device cannot keep up, so it stays smooth.
