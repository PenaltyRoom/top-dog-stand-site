Top Dog Stand

Privacy policy

Top Dog Stand is a game made by Fadil Ahmed ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play it. The short version: the game collects nothing about you. Last updated: 18 October 2026.

What the game collects

Nothing. There is no account or sign-in, no adverts, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking. The game never connects to the internet: everything it needs is inside the app.

What stays on your device

Your progress and settings (chef level, coins, stars, upgrades, hats and awnings, your franchise business, sound and vibration choices and the skin tone of your hands) are saved in the app’s own storage on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Apple

Apple runs the App Store and may collect information when you download or update the game, as described in Apple’s own privacy policy.

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device’s settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. We use them only to fix problems. You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, so it collects none from children.

Your rights

Data protection law, including the UK GDPR, gives you rights over personal information held about you. The game gives us none, so there is nothing for us to show, correct or delete. If you have any question about privacy, email us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the game and on our website with a new date.
