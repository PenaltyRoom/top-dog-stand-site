Top Dog Stand

Privacy policy

Top Dog Stand is a game made by Fadil Ahmed ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play it. The short version: the game itself collects nothing about you and never tracks you. Optional ads are shown by Google AdMob, which receives some technical information to show them, and purchases are handled by Apple. Last updated: 19 October 2026.

1. What stays on your device

Your progress and settings (chef level, coins, stars, upgrades, hats and awnings, your franchise business, the daily treat, sound and vibration choices, the skin tone of your hands, and whether you own No Ads) are saved in the app’s own storage on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

There is no account or sign-in, and the game has no analytics or tracking services of its own.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ads are always your choice: after the stand has closed, or on the daily treat, you can watch an ad for a bonus (double the day’s takings, try a day again with extra time, or an extra treat). No ad ever appears during play, and you never have to watch one. With No Ads, the bonuses come without ads and no ads are requested at all.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob through Google’s ad software built into the game (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). So that an ad is ready when you want one, the game asks Google for one in the background while your device is online.

Every ad request is tagged for child-directed treatment and as coming from someone under the age of consent, for every player whatever their age, and is limited to ads suitable for everyone (rated G). This tells Google to apply its strictest protections: no personalised ads, no remarketing, and the device’s advertising identifier is not sent.

Nothing is used to track you. The game never asks Apple’s “Allow tracking” question because it does not track: it never has your device’s advertising identifier, information from the game is never combined with information from other companies’ apps or websites to target or measure ads, and it is never shared with data brokers. It shows no tracking or ad-permission pop-ups, in any country.

What Google receives, and why. To show ads, Google’s ad software collects: your approximate location, worked out from your internet (IP) address; an identifier for your device that is not the advertising identifier; advertising data (which ads were requested, shown, tapped or watched to the end); and diagnostic data (crashes and performance of its software).

Google uses this only to deliver these non-personalised ads, limit how often you see the same ad, count and report ads in total, find and fix problems in its software, and prevent fraud and abuse. We never receive it, and none of it is linked to you by us. How Google handles it: policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

If an ad seems wrong for the game or for younger players, tap Settings → Report an ad. It opens an email to us with space to say what the ad showed and roughly when. We use AdMob’s controls to block that ad or advertiser where we can.

3. Purchases (Apple’s App Store)

The No Ads purchase is made through Apple’s App Store and follows Apple’s terms and privacy policy. We never see your payment details, your name or your Apple Account. On your device, Apple’s StoreKit tells the game the price in your currency and whether your Apple Account owns No Ads, so it can be unlocked and restored.

4. Who receives information

The game shares information with only two companies: Google, for the ads in section 2, and Apple, for App Store purchases in section 3. Each is bound by its own privacy policy and data protection terms to give your information the same or equal protection as this policy: Google at policies.google.com/privacy, Apple at www.apple.com/legal/privacy.

We do not sell or share your information with anyone else. If you have turned on Share With App Developers in your device’s Analytics settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage totals; they do not identify you. You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

5. Children

Top Dog Stand is a game for everyone (rated 4+); it is not made specifically for children. Because the game collects no personal information and treats every ad request as child-directed and under the age of consent, children can play without sharing any. Parents can turn off in-app purchases in Screen Time. If you think a child has sent us personal information (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

6. Keeping and deleting information

Your progress stays on your device until you delete the app. Purchases stay with your Apple Account.

Google keeps ad data under its own retention rules (policies.google.com/technologies/retention). To ask Google about it or have it deleted, use policies.google.com/privacy, or contact us and we will pass your request on.

The game plays without an internet connection; while it is offline, no ads are requested and nothing is sent to Google.

7. Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to how it is used. We hold none from the game itself. For information Google’s ad software received, contact Google through policies.google.com/privacy, or contact us and we will pass your request on. You can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk) or your local data protection authority.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the game and on our website with a new date.
